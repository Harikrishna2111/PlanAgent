About This Framework

This is a 50% prototype of the Agentic AI Framework for Constraint-Aware Architectural Design and BIM-Based Decision Support. It demonstrates the core pipeline: natural-language understanding, spatial reasoning, procedural generation, constraint evaluation, and iterative optimization.

Natural Language Understanding

Parse complex architectural briefs into structured requirements

Spatial Reasoning

Build relationship graphs to model room adjacencies and separations

Simulated Annealing

Iterative optimization that refines floor plans toward global optima

8-Dimension Scoring

Comprehensive spatial quality evaluation across multiple criteria
